two things the mockup cannot decide

A launch screen cannot know it is a first run.
On iOS it is a static launch storyboard and on Android a windowBackground — both drawn by the system before any code of ours runs. Neither can read hasCompletedFirstRun. So this is the splash on every launch, and "first run" stays what it already is: the Welcome behind it. If a splash that appears only once is what is wanted, it has to be an in-app screen, and then it is a second welcome.
macOS may not want one at all.
Chotki opens as a popover from the menu bar; a popover that shows a splash and then swaps to content flickers in a way a full window does not. The popover frame above is drawn to show the composition at that size, not to argue it belongs there. The window with the Dock icon is the surface where a splash would make sense, if either does.
