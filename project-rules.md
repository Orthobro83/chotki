A record that already stored a church and has no affiliation field keeps that church. A church with no published Akathist dates uses the OCA's, and says so. The appointments and the sources are in `church-practice.md`. Apply this on Android and Mac now, with the iOS design overhaul later, then the Windows port.

- 2026-10-06: The church calendar for 2026–2031 ships inside the app on macOS, iOS and Windows, generated from Orthocal's MIT code (the maintainer permitted its use) and audited. - 2026-10-06: The prayer rope is a ring with a bead between every ten knots; the first bead is both start and end, filled from the start, and the chime sounds when the count returns to it. A bead is an added mark, never a knot with a different face. The knot before a bead sounds a lower "tock" instead of the tick. Where the marks sit, and which sound a counted knot makes, are decided in core (`RopeCircleLayout`, `PrayerScreen.cue`) and the platforms only draw and play them.
- 2026-10-06: A link back to today appears once the calendar has moved away from it, "← Today" on the left in the future and "Today →" on the right in the past (core's `TodayLink`); Windows keeps its own Today button.
