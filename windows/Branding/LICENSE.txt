A NOTE ON THE CONTENT

The liturgical data shown in this application is retrieved from orthocal.info
and belongs to its authors. The passages from the Church Fathers are drawn from
translations published between 1885 and 1900, which are in the public domain.
The prayers are from older public-domain liturgical English, chiefly the
Hapgood Service Book of 1906. Where the application links to prayers published
by parishes and jurisdictions, those are references to read, not the source of
any wording reproduced here — their translations remain the property of their
publishers.
The glossary and other explanatory text are the author's own and carry the same
terms as the software.

Nothing in this application is authoritative on matters of faith or practice.
It has not been reviewed or approved by any ecclesiastical authority.
